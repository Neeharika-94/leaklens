How it works

Claude: intelligence

The accountant gives a statement to Claude. Claude identifies merchants, spots duplicates, unused services, FX and idle cards, and explains each in plain English.

LeakLens: accountability

Receives only structured findings through MCP. Deterministic calculator (no AI), accountant approval, shareable founder report, savings tracked from potential to realised.

LeakLens contains no AI of its own. Claude's confidence is never treated as financial verification: the accountant approves every number.
